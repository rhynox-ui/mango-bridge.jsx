Mango Protocol is a permissionless infrastructure suite for moving assets across chains and launching new tokens. Anyone can bridge, anyone can launch a token, anyone can trade — there's no gatekeeping, no approval process, no account to register. You connect a wallet and use it.

The app is live at [mangoprotocol.site](https://mangoprotocol.site), with the bridge live on mainnet across:

- Ethereum
- Base
- BNB Chain
- Robinhood Chain
- Stable (Tether's own L1, native gas token USDT0)
- Solana
- Arbitrum One
- Avalanche
- Abstract
- HyperEVM
- Ink
- Plasma
- Unichain
- X Layer

Launchpad contracts are deployed and verified on Robinhood Chain mainnet; the launch and trading interface is still in development.

This blog is where we'll post real updates as they ship — new chain support, new features, and anything else worth a longer explanation than a tweet. No hype, no fabricated numbers — if something's still in progress, we'll say so.

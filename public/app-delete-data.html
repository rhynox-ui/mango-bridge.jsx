Delete My Data

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Mango has no traditional user account, so there's almost nothing on our end to delete: your recovery phrase, private keys, and transaction history live entirely on your own device and were never sent to Mango's servers. The one thing Mango's servers do store is described below, and there are two ways to delete it.

What this covers

If you've used the Mango app's Referral feature, Mango's servers store a small record tied to your public wallet address: your accumulated referral points, who referred you (if anyone), how many people you've referred, and your daily check-in status. See the Privacy Policy's "What we collect" section for the full picture — this is the only server-side data the app creates.

This does not cover your wallet itself. Your recovery phrase, private keys, and password are generated and encrypted entirely on your device and were never sent to Mango in any form — there's nothing server-side to delete for those. To remove wallet data from your device, uninstall the app or use Reset wallet in Settings.

Option 1: Delete it in the app (fastest)

Open the Mango app → Settings → About Mango → Delete My Data. Confirm the prompt, then confirm with your biometrics or wallet password. The app signs a message with your wallet proving you own that address, and sends it directly to Mango's server, which deletes your referral record immediately — no waiting, no email round-trip.

Option 2: Request it by email

If you'd rather not do this from the app, email mango@mangoprotocol.site from any address, including the wallet address you used, and request deletion. We'll permanently delete that referral record within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your referral points balance and referral count.
  • Your daily check-in cooldown status.
  • The record of who referred you.

This is permanent and cannot be undone. Referral rewards someone else already received because you referred them aren't reversed — the same way deleting your own bank statement doesn't undo a payment someone else already received from you.

Contact

Questions about this can be sent to mango@mangoprotocol.site, or through Mango's usual channels at mangoprotocol.site.
